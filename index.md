## About

I am a PhD student in Mathematical Sciences at Carnegie Mellon University. My research interests lie at the intersection of stochastic analysis, partial differential equations, and machine learning.

My work spans scientific machine learning, reinforcement learning for stochastic control, and quantitative research. I am especially interested in problems where mathematical structure can make learning, control, and decision-making more reliable.

## Research interests

- **Scientific machine learning:** Physics-informed neural networks, diffusion and flow-based models, and numerical methods for scientific problems.
- **Stochastic systems and control:** Reinforcement learning and PDE methods for stochastic impulse control and diffusion processes.
- **Quantitative research:** Dynamic allocation, risk management, stochastic calculus, and portfolio decisions under uncertainty.
